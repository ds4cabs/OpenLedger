PFIZER INC. — FORM 10-K (sample excerpt)

Results of Operations — Revenues

Our revenues are derived from the sale of biopharmaceutical products. The following
discussion summarizes revenues for our principal products for the year ended
December 31.

Comirnaty contributed revenues of $11,220 million, reflecting lower demand for
COVID-19 vaccines compared to the prior year. Comirnaty remains one of our largest
products by revenue.

Paxlovid generated revenues of $1,279 million for the year, down from prior-year
levels as pandemic-related demand normalized.

Eliquis revenues were $6,747 million, an increase driven by continued oral
anticoagulant market growth. Eliquis is recorded under our alliance revenues with
Bristol-Myers Squibb.

Prevnar family revenues were $6,440 million, supported by adult and pediatric
pneumococcal vaccination.

Ibrance generated revenues of $4,753 million in metastatic breast cancer, with
continued competition from other CDK 4/6 inhibitors.

Vyndaqel/Vyndamax revenues grew to $3,316 million, reflecting strong uptake in
transthyretin amyloid cardiomyopathy.

Total company revenues for the year were $58,496 million.

Patents and Exclusivity

Certain products face loss of exclusivity over the next several years, which could
materially affect revenues. Eliquis composition-of-matter protection and Ibrance
exclusivity are among the key dates investors monitor.
